Use cases

Vitalism features

Six ways the Vitalism application supports care, research and everyday wellbeing.

01

Remote patient monitoring

The Vitalism system enables remote monitoring of patients’ vital signs, so doctors and caregivers can keep track of their patients’ health status without in-person visits. This is particularly useful for patients with chronic conditions, elderly patients, and patients who live in remote areas.

02

Elderly care

The Vitalism application can be used to care for elderly individuals. It continuously monitors their vital signs, allowing caregivers to respond quickly to any emergency or change in health status.

03

Telehealth

Patients can consult their healthcare provider through the Vitalism application. The provider can view the patient’s vital signs in real time, make informed decisions about the patient’s health, and provide guidance accordingly.

04

Virtual trials

Researchers can use the Vitalism application to access data for research studies — collecting it in a non-invasive way while still gaining valuable insights into the health and wellness of participants.

05

Medication adherence

Healthcare providers and caregivers can use the Vitalism application to monitor a patient’s medication adherence. The application can send reminders to the patient, or alert the provider if the patient is not taking their medication as prescribed.

06

Patient triage

Patients can use the Vitalism application to track their vital signs and assess their health status — staying informed about their health and seeking medical attention when necessary.

New capability

Stress level monitoring from recorded videos

The Vitalism application extends vital-sign measurement to stress analysis in both recorded and live videos. It assesses individual stress through heartbeat and heart rate variability (HRV), and also monitors audience reactions in group settings to evaluate engagement and tension.

Key features include:

  • Stress-level extraction using remote photoplethysmography (rPPG) and wavelet filtering
  • Speech-content analysis for stress indicators
  • Facial-expression recognition to detect emotional cues

The system also considers contextual factors such as time and environment.

A notable use case is our analysis of the Trump–Clinton debate, where stress levels were compared based on pulse-rate data.
